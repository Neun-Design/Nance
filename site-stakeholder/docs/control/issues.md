# Issues

**What it is:** the positive and negative factors the organisation decides to carry forward
(ISO 9001:2015 §4.1/§4.2). Positive ones are **Opportunities**, negative ones are **Risks**.
An Issue is the output of governance investigation — what you concluded after looking at a
process or procedure problem — and the input to the next modelling cycle: it justifies a new
[Scope](../portfolio/scopes.md), a new [Requirement](../operation/requirements.md), a new
[Event](../portfolio/events.md) or a revised [Procedure](../operation/procedures.md).

**Register when:** whenever an investigation concludes. In the first modelling pass that
means registering the strategic opportunities that justify your scopes, before
[Scopes](../portfolio/scopes.md) (phase 3). From then on, Issues accumulate continuously as
[Performance](performance.md) and nonconformity investigation surface them — risks will be
elevated automatically once the nonconformity flow ships.

**Key fields:** Name, Type (`Opportunity` / `Risk`), `Unit` (grouped by segment), Owner.

!!! note "No dashboard tab yet"
    Issues is a **hidden registry**: it has no tab of its own in the app. Register one with
    the "+" button on the Opportunity field of the [Scopes](../portfolio/scopes.md) form.
    The dedicated Control tab arrives with the Nonconformity flow — see
    [Performance](performance.md).
