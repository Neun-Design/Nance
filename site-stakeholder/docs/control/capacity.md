# Capacity

**What it is:** how much the organisation *can* execute. The view aggregates role and
department capacity from [People](../talent/people.md), their working hours and their
[Squads](../organization/squads.md).

**Register when:** never — this is a computed view. Its accuracy depends entirely on the
Talent and Organization modules being complete: a person with no squad, or a role with no
skill level, is a hole in the capacity picture.

**What to look for:** the gap between capacity and the demand carried by
[Tickets](../workspace/tickets.md) is the first place a governance problem shows up — an
under-resourced department is a risk waiting to be registered as an [Issue](issues.md).
