# Business Segments

**What it is:** the top-level market segments (e.g. Diagnostic Imaging, Clinical Analysis, Outpatient Care).
**Register when:** first thing, phase 1 — one row per segment.
**Key fields:** Name, Acronym, Owner (accountability, ISO §5.3).
