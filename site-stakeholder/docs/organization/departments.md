# Departments

**What it is:** the service departments of a unit (e.g. Radiology Operations, Diagnostic Reporting).
**Register when:** after Units — Events and People will need them.
**Key fields:** `Business Unit *`, Name, Acronym, Manager. A department automatically appears in
the drill-down of every Branch of its unit. Expanding a department shows its **Squads** and
(2026-09-08) the **Events** it answers — each [Event](../portfolio/events.md) is a request
one department must fulfill for its clients.
