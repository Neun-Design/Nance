# Roles

**What it is:** a job title inside a function, exercised in one or more departments. The
role carries **no skill level and no job family** (2026-09-07): the level is defined per
[competence](competence.md) — the competence owns the role + level pair, and a person
accumulates those pairs through onboarding — and the family inherits through the
[function](functions.md). This avoids near-duplicate roles that differed only by level
(e.g. *Designer Junior* / *Designer Senior*), and a person keeps their competences when
they change function.
**Register when:** after Functions (departments come from Organization).
**Key fields:** Name; `Business Unit *` (2026-09-07) — pick the unit first and the
Function options narrow to it (until role-based access control lands, forms carry
filter inputs like this one so the same MVP serves different business segments and
units); `Function *` (the selected unit's functions) → unlocks **Departments** (multi —
the departments of the function's business units; the by-unit grouping was dropped
since the unit is now chosen on the form); Quantity (headcount).
