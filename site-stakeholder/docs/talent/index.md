# Talent

People, their functions and the certified competences that gate execution (ISO 9001:2015 §7.2).
Phase 4 covers the people basics; **Competence and Onboarding are phase 6** — they need the
Events registered in [Operation](../operation/index.md).

## Entities in this module

Listed in registration order. Each page answers three questions: *what it is*, *when to register one*, and *the key fields* — including which selection unlocks which.

- [**Skill Levels**](skill-levels.md) — proficiency levels required by roles (Junior/Senior/Expert). The rank is not registered here — it is the professional's step inside a level, recorded per competence (Skill Rank 1–3 on the Competence…
- [**Job Family**](job-family.md) — formal education records (title, field, institution).
- [**Functions**](functions.md) — job functions inside a business unit (e.g. Design Engineer).
- [**Roles**](roles.md) — a function at a skill level with a graduation — the unit of competence requirements.
- [**People**](people.md) — the people registry — every Owner/Manager selector in the app points here.
- [**Competence**](competence.md) — a certified capability: a role/level certified for a task chain (event → process → task) on a product scope (the scope × product-group pair in one key — the separate Scope / Product Group selects are…
- [**Onboarding**](onboarding.md) — the certification record of a person on a competence (with training material and the certified flag) — Jobs only offer certified people as responsibles.
