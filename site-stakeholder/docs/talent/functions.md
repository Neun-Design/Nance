# Functions

**What it is:** job functions inside a business unit (e.g. Design Engineer).
**Register when:** after Units.
**Key fields:** Name; `Business Unit *`; **Job Family** (2026-08-28) — the
[family](job-family.md) this function belongs to, picked here once: people inherit it
through their function (options grouped by the family's field); Owner.
